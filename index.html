What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. TripWise has no accounts, no forms that send data to us, no analytics, no advertising and no server of our own.

What stays on your device

If you add countries to your trip, the list is saved in your browser’s local storage so it is there next time. We also save the country list there so the page loads faster and works offline. We cannot see this data. Choose “Remove all stops” or clear this site’s data in your browser to delete it.

Third-party services

To show results, your browser connects directly to these services, which receive your IP address and browser details, as with any web request: REST Countries (country data), jsDelivr and GitHub (backup country data), Flagcdn (flag images), Wikipedia (descriptions) and Open-Meteo (weather). Wikipedia and Open-Meteo are contacted only when you open a country. We send them no other information.
